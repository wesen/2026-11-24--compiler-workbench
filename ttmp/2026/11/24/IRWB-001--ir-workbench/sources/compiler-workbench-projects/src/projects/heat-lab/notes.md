`ui/heat.js` runs the current image on an input the sliders shape and annotates three views: instruction counts in the source gutter, a shade on hot lines, and badges on the hottest instructions in RV32. `ui/fragments.js` adds `Table.heat(column)` and `Stat.share(of)` to the built-in components with a UI fragment; `ui/compare.js` uses both on the result of `scripts/compare.js`, which runs the input on a plain backend (no compare fusing, no immediates, four registers) and on the tuned one.
