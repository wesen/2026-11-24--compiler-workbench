# Tix language, tensor IR, evaluators, contract and passes

Tix is the first tensor domain in IR Workbench. Stage 1 covers parsing and shape checking, a v6 artifact, f32 and f64 evaluation, checked inlining and sum splitting, and six corpus programs. The design is ready for review before implementation.
