# Scratchpad archive (2026-11-26 to 2026-11-27)

These are the programs and scripts that were written in the coordinator's session scratchpad and never copied into a ticket. They are archived here, at their scratchpad paths, on the user's instruction (2026-11-27): "save temporary programs and scripts in the scripts/ folder of the relevant ticket … so that we don't lose important parts".

The scratchpad scripts that the ticket already held, under other names, are not repeated:
- `t1-acceptance/*`: the frozen stage-1 suite;
- `t2-eval/t2-eval.mjs`: the hidden stage-2 evaluator, `various/hidden-t2/`;
- `exact-probe/*`: `various/exact-probe/`;
- `eff-probe/main.cu`: `various/eff-probe/`.

Paths inside the scripts point at the scratchpad, at worktrees or at `dist/`, as they were run. Most take the repository or a worktree root as their first argument.
