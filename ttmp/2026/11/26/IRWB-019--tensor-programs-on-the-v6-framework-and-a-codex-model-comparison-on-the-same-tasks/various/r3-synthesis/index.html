What forty implementations say about the framework

Four agents reviewed the IR framework against two compilers (Typed to RV32, Tix to CUDA) and 31 unmerged implementations: Sol and Astra (OpenAI Codex), Opus and Fable (Claude). This page verifies their claims with probes, merges their proposals into one target architecture and one standard library, and orders a migration.

Verdict
  1. The outer shell held. Content-addressed revisions, adapters and projections, the contract vocabulary, revision-exact analysis caching, the batched differential and the strategy plan language carried both domains and every cell.
  2. The inner model of a transformation did not. A pass is an endomorphism. Its record is a diff. "Checked" is one word. Acceptance is spread over two runners and several publication points. Independent agents routed around each of these in the same few shapes.
  3. The coordinator was wrong about F27. Tix passes run in strategy plans today. They lose the meaning gate, and the bridge's existing contract option restores it without a framework change.
  4. v7 has six core concepts: a typed transformation with one lifecycle; a derivation written by the producer; evidence as relation × method × scope; staged publication; one runner with two drivers; decisions as data. A standard library of about twenty primitives sits below them.
  5. First moves: honest evidence, atomic publication, Tix plans through the bridge contract, then one runner.

The target architecture

The core knows nothing about programs. The standard library holds algorithms over one shape each (a graph, a lattice, terms, affine forms, binary32) and registers with the core only through optional wrappers. Domains supply semantics: evaluators, native transactions, emitters, measured constants.

K1 · Typed transformation evidenced

Ports in, ports out; an endomorphic pass is the special case. Outcomes are accepted, unchanged, refused, rejected, unavailable, pending.

Theory Hoare triples; translation validation; composition of relations.

Absorbs PassSpec, T6's legal/postcondition/source, the CUDA lowering's special shape, lowerToImage.

K2 · Derivation evidenced

The producer records steps: what it consumed and produced, the rule or decision, the premises. Correspondence is computed from the steps with a coverage policy, and the diff audits it.

Theory Proof terms of rewriting; witnessing transformations; provenance semirings.

Absorbs revisionCorrespondence, seven A0 re-derivers, seven D1 lineage walks, F30, F31.

K3 · Evidence gap evidenced algebra conjecture

A relation (exact, up to NaN class, within a bound, under an assumption), a method (sampled, exhaustive, by construction, certified) and a scope. Unknown evidence is never exact.

Absorbs the single 'checked', assuranceOf, the relation chosen by pass name.

K4 · Staged publication evidenced

Prepare privately, validate, then publish every output or none. Start from the manager's deferCommit.

Absorbs the store retain during preparation, and the commit before the preservation checks.

K5 · One runner, two drivers runner plans

A generator that requests analyses, gates and batches; a sync driver and an async driver answer. The manager needs a synchronous path for pure analyses.

Absorbs applyNativePassSync's limits, both T6 sync.ts, optimizeTix's loop.

K6 · Decisions as data split one signature

Untrusted decide returns a plan; a small eligible(plan) decides legality; prepare applies it. Fusion, schedules, binding-time divisions and guarded rewrites are instances.

Plus staged choice: a launch-time variant with its guard, so the differential's arms are derived.

Kept out of the core on purpose: graph algorithms, SSA, affine dependence, specialization, f32, a universal IR, an e-graph, a polyhedral library and an SMT solver. The core must be stated without saying what a program is, and it must run with an empty library.

The standard library

Admission: a named algorithm or law; input is a small shape interface, never a domain type; two clients (two IR levels of one compiler count); an executable checker or fixture; pure and synchronous, with an optional registration wrapper. Promotion test: name both clients, the law, the code to retire, and one input on which a careless extraction changes meaning.

The migration

Each step is small, keeps the suites green and deletes something. Amber lines are the decision points where a measurement can withdraw a proposal.

  1. Baselines. Framework reach, the edit and correspondence census, ms per application, and the share of accepted steps by evidence method.Probes 05, 09, 11.
  2. Honest evidence. Records carry method and scope; unknown evidence is unknown; Typed's rule strength flows into a pass's evidence.Deletes the 'checked' constant and assuranceOf's default. Check: probe 01 prints sampled and exhaustive.
  3. Atomic publication. Prepare candidates on a private view; commit nothing before the contract and the preservation checks pass.Deletes the retain at view.ts:190 during preparation. Check: probe 03 leaves the head and the facts untouched.
  4. Tix plans now (F27 restated). The meaning gate becomes a bridge contract; optimizeTix runs a plan.Deletes the name dispatch at tensor/index.ts:32-60 and the case for executeSync. Check: identical revisions; a wrong pass is rejected.
  5. One pass runner, two drivers. A generator runner, and analyseSync for pure analyses.Deletes the restriction at define-pass.ts:231.Decision: if analyseSync duplicates the manager's cache logic, keep one spec with two thin runners.
  6. Derivation and scoped correspondence. Tx.record writes rule ids; Tix inline records merged; coverage is checked, and the diff audits it.Check: rule-citing records go from 0 to 183; empty and false mappings are rejected.Decision: if a derivation costs more than about 2× today's correspondence, store local terms only on demand.
  7. Cross-IR transformations. Tix → CUDA and lowerToImage on K1.Check: the composed correspondence equals today's; image bytes unchanged.
  8. The first library. Liveness for the allocator and the coalescer, then graph, lattice and ssa, moved, not rewritten.Check: byte-identical images; unreachable-block scope.Decision: if an adapter is larger than the algorithm it replaces, keep the domain copy.
  9. Attribution and classification as queries.Check: the merged D1 tables reproduce; both A0 policies (203 and 395) come from one record.
  10. Fusion as decide, eligible, prepare, with the affine library; merge T6 on it. A manifest drives the emitter and the runner's gate names.Deletes the gate regex at cuda-runtime.ts:142. Check: byte-identical CUDA; the extent-17 counterexample.Decision: if fusion and scheduling need different eligible signatures, withdraw K6's unification.
  11. solveFor and pe. Port SCCP; restate one B1 specializer.Decision: if the restatement is not at most half of the cell's 719 lines, unfold is not earning its place.
  12. Rules over a term signature. Typed simplify and fold; Tix inline and split-sum.Decision: if the rule path is materially slower than the compiled visitor, share only the matcher and the verifier.
  13. Keep or move the rewrite engine, the transaction layer and the 1,237-line core runtime.Decision: adopt the runtime only if its per-edit checking cost falls with program size (E1 benchmark).
  14. Staged choice. The fusion gate as a launch-time select, with differential arms derived.

The four reviews

I am a Claude model judging two Claude reviews (Opus, Fable) and two OpenAI reviews (Sol, Astra). Earlier rounds showed that judges favour their own family, so this assessment rests on what the probes verified, not on how a review reads.

A · Sol

Found the manager accepts a fact before the pass rejects (P6); a reuse key is a dependency claim; affine exactness.

Errors none found. Probes held P1, P6.

Deep on acceptance semantics; dense to read.

B · Astra

Found T6-D's validator miss; zero groups accepted; the rejected store head; universe as part of the type; precondition ≠ validator.

Errors none found. Probes held 01, 05.

The sharpest counterexamples; a narrower inventory.

C · Opus

Found F27's real cause; the broadest inventory (U1–U22); overlay IR; staged choice; refines.

Errors "the record does not say so" is too strong; the BTA probe is not on Tix.

The most readable; fewer probes.

D · Fable

Found a BTA on Tix through the manager; fusion as distance vectors; the generator runner; the corpus census; the on-demand solver.

Errors the 2,021/1,774 reason; 98.5 % read as a defect; diff.ts listed as unused.

The most "it fits" probes; the largest catalogue (34 primitives).

What survives if style is discounted. Four conclusions rest on verified probes, and each is carried by at least one review from each family:

  • the transformation must carry its own obligations and outcomes;
  • evidence must state method and scope;
  • correspondence must come from the pass and be checked for coverage;
  • most of the inventory is a library problem.

Only the two OpenAI reviews found the publication defect, and it held. The conclusions resting on a single Claude review are the ones marked conjecture: overlay IR, staged choice, the on-demand solver, and the decide/eligible unification.
