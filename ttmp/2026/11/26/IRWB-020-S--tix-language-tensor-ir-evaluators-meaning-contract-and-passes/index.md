# Tix language, tensor IR, evaluators, meaning contract and passes

## Overview

Stage 1 brings a tensor index language, native v6 IR, f32 and f64 evaluators, and two meaning-checked passes to the existing IR framework. The goal is to identify what v6 already supports and what a second domain needs changed. The design is ready for review; implementation has not started.
