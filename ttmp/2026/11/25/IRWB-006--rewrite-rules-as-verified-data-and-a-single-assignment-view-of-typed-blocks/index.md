# Rewrite rules as verified data and a single-assignment view of typed.blocks

## Overview

Items B1, B2 and A1 of the IRWB-002 agenda, plus a first A2 experiment:

- **B1, rules as data.** `simplify` (27 rules) and `fold` (40 rules) are rule tables (`src/typed/rules/`), compiled into a `Tx.rewrite` visitor. The table passes give identical revisions, edit lists and correspondences on every corpus pipeline and on Candela's O2; `passes.ts` now uses them. The hand-written bodies are kept in `src/typed/rules/handwritten.ts` for the regression test.
- **B2, verified once.** `src/typed/rules/verify.ts` checks each rule with eight methods of stated strength; the evidence is recorded in `src/typed/rules/evidence.ts` (generated by `scripts/04-verify-rules.mjs`) and pinned by fingerprint. Deliberately unsound candidates (`src/typed/rules/candidates.ts`) are rejected in `tests/typed/rules.test.mjs`.
- **A1, SSA view.** `typed.ssa` (`src/typed/ssa.ts`): dominance frontiers, minimal phis, renaming, the mapping back to statements and values; agrees with reaching definitions on every corpus revision and on Candela r0.
- **A2, first consumer.** `sccp` and the pipeline `O2-sccp` (`src/typed/sccp.ts`), outside `PASSES` and `PIPELINES`.
